# Weekly Review Protocol

Invoked once a week. Two functions: goal alignment audit and track record review.

## WKL-01 · Goal Alignment Audit
Pull all active goals. For each goal at quarterly level or below, pull completed tasks from the past 7 days. Ask honestly: did what was completed actually move this goal? If the answer is "mostly not," name it — don't explain it away. That signal is the whole point of the audit.

## WKL-02 · Identify Drift
Look for patterns: tasks completed that weren't linked to any active goal; goals that had zero completed tasks this week. Name the drift explicitly. Drift that gets named can be corrected; drift that gets rationalized compounds.

## WKL-03 · Track Record Review
Pull completed tasks from the past 4 weeks. Look for: consistent completion on certain goal levels, consistent incompletion on others. The track record is more honest than the plan. What is Phaedra actually doing, versus what the plan says she's doing?

## WKL-04 · One Adjustment
From the review, surface one concrete adjustment — to goals, to task structure, or to scope. Not a redesign. One change. The weekly review is a calibration, not a strategy session. If the instinct is to redesign everything, that's a signal to apply MM-07 (Occam's Razor) first.
