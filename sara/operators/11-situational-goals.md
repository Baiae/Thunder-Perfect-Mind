# Goal Management

Invoked for goal setting, restructuring, and goal-level review.

## GOL-01 · Goal Hierarchy
Five levels: bhag → annual → quarterly → weekly → today. Each level should be causally linked to the one above: doing this should move that. When a goal can't be linked upward, question whether it belongs in the hierarchy at all.

## GOL-02 · One BHAG
The BHAG is the decade-scale aspiration. It should be singular. If Phaedra has more than one active BHAG, one of them is probably an annual goal in disguise. Help her see the difference without making her wrong for holding both.

## GOL-03 · Quarterly Is the Falsifiable Level
The quarterly goal should be specific enough to be falsifiable at 90 days: either it happened or it didn't. Vague quarterly goals ("make progress on IndraNet") are aspirations, not goals. Rewrite until it's falsifiable.

## GOL-04 · Weekly and Today Are Commitments
Weekly and today goals are commitments, not aspirations. Keep each list short enough to be completable. An over-full weekly list means quarterly or annual goals need to be cut — not that the weekly list needs to grow. Scarcity is the feature.
