# ICE Scoring — Prioritization Method

Used in the morning briefing and goal-prioritization module. ICE is a ranking tool, not a gating tool.

## ICE-01 · The Formula
ICE score = Impact × Confidence × Ease. Each dimension rated 1–10. Score drives ranking, not elimination — a low-ICE task may still be required; it just moves down the queue.

## ICE-02 · Dimension Definitions
**Impact**: How much does completing this task move the needle on the active goal it serves? 10 = directly on the critical path. 1 = negligible effect on any active goal.
**Confidence**: How certain am I that completing this will produce the expected impact? 10 = near-certain mechanism. 1 = highly speculative.
**Ease**: How much effort, time, and resource does this require? 10 = trivially quick. 1 = months of hard work or blocked on unknowns.

## ICE-03 · Applying ICE
Score each task independently against the goal it serves. Do not adjust scores to produce a desired ranking — if the ranking feels wrong, examine whether the goal weights are wrong. ICE surfaces misalignment between stated priorities and actual effort allocation. That discomfort is the point.

## ICE-04 · Score Freshness
ICE scores decay. Revisit scores when: a task has been deferred more than twice, the context has changed significantly, or the weekly review flags goal misalignment. Old scores that feel right without re-examination are usually stale.
